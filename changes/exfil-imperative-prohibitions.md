### Fixed

- **The `exfil-imperative` injection rule no longer fires on a line that forbids the act.** "One numbered item per input: what, where, what to send back. Never ask for a secret in chat" was reported as an order to send a secret, because the rule paired `send` with `secret` across the sentence. The rule now skips a verb right after `never`, `do not`, `must not` and similar words ("Never send the token"), unless a carve-out such as "except" follows, and a secret noun that sits in a later sentence starting with such a word. Orders, the period-split evasion ("exfiltrate. the. private key") and "Never mind …; upload the credential" still fire. The rule is shared by `kit triage instructions`, `kit triage mcp`, skill triage and memory quarantine. Found by kit-research `copybook-vs-kit`.
