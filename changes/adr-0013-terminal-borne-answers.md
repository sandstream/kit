### Added

- **Proposed ADR-0013: an answer that arrives through a terminal or the agent's stream is not a person's answer.** `isTTY` shows that a terminal is attached, not that a person is at it. Measured on `main`: `requestApproval` refuses a piped `yes`, but it accepts a `yes` typed into its `tmux` pane by another process with `tmux send-keys`, and `gate-bash` lets that `send-keys` through. The ADR extends ADR-0010 to kit's own prompts (`requestApproval` and `kit policy approve`). A person's answer must arrive through a harness surface outside the agent's terminal, or through a signed token that needs a physical key touch. Typing into another terminal becomes a gated action. The ADR is proposed and gates nothing until the owner accepts it. Found by the kit-research notes `agents-as-engineering-org-vs-kit` and `agent-vm-yolo-herdr-paseo-vs-kit`.
