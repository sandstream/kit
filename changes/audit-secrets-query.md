### Added

- **`kit audit secrets --key <NAME> --since <ISO> [--json]` answers "was NAME read or written through kit since T, and by whom", from the audit chain, and says what it cannot see.** It lists each read (`secrets.read`, `secrets.generate`, `secrets.incident`) and write (`propagate`, `sync`, `rotate`, `migrate`, `onecli register`) of the key since T with the declared agent, the harness kit detected and the kit surface, and counts the secret events in the window that name no key. It verifies the hash chain and the HMAC anchor first and prints whether they verified; when they do not, when the log is missing, or when `--since` is not ISO 8601, it exits 1. Every answer states its coverage: reads through kit only; processes that read `.env.local` directly are not visible. Found by kit-research note kms-decrypt-evidence-vs-kit.
